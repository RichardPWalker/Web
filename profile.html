About Richard Walker

Strategic Parking Consultancy brings together practical operational experience and a wider strategic view of how parking can support places, people and local economies.

Richard Walker is a parking-sector leader with experience in local-authority parking services, service delivery, partnership working and strategic planning. His work has involved balancing the day-to-day realities of running parking services with the longer-term priorities of transport, town-centre vitality, customer experience and financial sustainability.

He understands parking as more than the management of spaces or enforcement. Done well, it is part of how a place works: connecting people with destinations, supporting businesses, helping manage demand and contributing to wider transport objectives.

A practical, collaborative perspective

Richard’s approach combines evidence-led thinking with an understanding of front-line delivery. He values clear communication, proportionate solutions, collaboration and ideas that can be put into practice—not simply written into a strategy.
